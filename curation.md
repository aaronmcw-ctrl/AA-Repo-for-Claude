### Dawson Trotman
Dawson Trotman founded The Navigators and helped recover simple, forgotten practices of the Christian life: time in the Word, Scripture memory, prayer, personal follow-up and one-on-one discipleship. His talks are short and urgent, about reproducing your faith in others (“Born to Reproduce”), world vision, and believing God for big things.

### Ruth Myers
Ruth Myers served with The Navigators and as a missionary in Singapore alongside her husband Warren, and wrote The Satisfied Heart. Her messages are warm and practical, often for women, on God's love, emotions and healing, self-image, dating, courtship and marriage.

### LeRoy Eims
LeRoy Eims served with The Navigators for over 50 years, including as Director of U.S. Ministries and of Evangelism Worldwide, and wrote 14 books including The Lost Art of Disciplemaking. His large library covers discipleship, the basics of the Christian life, wisdom, the lordship of Christ and witnessing, plus a walk through the whole Bible and the daily 4-minute capsules.

### Irma Warr  ·  **DRAFT**
Irma Warr is best known in this library for The Godly Woman, a 23-part study for women. Her talks focus on marriage and the home, forgiveness, obedience and the lordship of Christ, with practical help for women growing in their walk with God.

### Jerry Bridges
Jerry Bridges joined The Navigators staff in 1955 and wrote The Pursuit of Holiness, The Pursuit of Godliness and The Discipline of Grace. His talks dig into holiness, grace, the character of God, true fellowship and the Spirit-filled life: careful teaching on how grace changes everyday living.

### Kirkie Morrissey
Kirkie Morrissey speaks at local and national conferences, teaches a class at her church and has written several books. Gentle but powerful, she draws on her own hard seasons; her talks explore faith, joy, the resurrection, the character of God and studies in Isaiah, often for women.

### Lorne Sanny
Lorne Sanny was mentored by Dawson Trotman and led The Navigators as General Director for 30 years. His teaching centers on discipleship and spiritual multiplication, the Word of God, leadership and ministry: practical wisdom from a lifetime of making disciples.

### Jim Downing
After a 24-year Navy career, Jim Downing served on The Navigators staff in many roles, including Deputy President and Chairman of the Board. His talks focus on the devotional life, abiding in and applying God's Word, prayer, the second coming, and growing to maturity as a laborer for Christ.
